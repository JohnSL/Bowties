Signaling facilities — Mast, Plant, and Line

A clean storyboard for the facility-first direction: add a named facility using the existing entry point, show Bowties the railroad, and let Bowties derive the repeated signal relationships.

Exploratory
This mockup tests a three-way entrance, not a settled domain model. Signal Mast and Signal Plant retain their established meanings. Signal Line is the working name for automatic signals along plain track; the mockup tests whether modelers recognize that job before Bowties commits to a persistent Line or Territory object.

Bowties already creates facilities by choosing a behavior template and entering a name. This mockup adds three signaling templates alongside the existing Block Indicator; the selected signaling template opens its dedicated wizard after the facility is added.

4. Signal Line — automatic signals along plain track

A Line is a sequence rather than a location. The user orders detected blocks; Bowties derives the boundary masts and their downstream cascade. Bidirectional use reveals the additional coordination that distinguishes APB from a simple directional ABS chain.

Bidirectional operation requires an Owner above individual Masts for direction latching, opposing-signal lockout, and release. The final name—Signal Line, Signaled Track, or Territory—remains a decision under test.
APB-like behavior coordinates both chains; it is not merely twice as many independent Masts.

5. How the three facility scales connect

Ownership remains singular at the boundaries. A Mast may read a Plant entrance as its downstream signal, but it does not become part of two facilities.

Ownership rule

Signal Mast owns

One combined indication and its physical display.
  • Protected block
  • Optional local turnout
  • Next-signal cascade

Signal Plant owns

The coordinated behavior at localized trackwork.
  • Home signals
  • Routes and conflicts
  • Shared turnout bindings

Signal Line may own

The ordered relationship along plain track.
  • Block sequence
  • Generated Mast chain
  • APB direction coordination

6. Decisions this mockup is testing

The storyboard is useful before every domain name and persistence rule is settled. These are the specific conclusions a review should confirm or overturn.

Review guide
DecisionCurrent directionStatus
First question Choose one signal, one coordinated location, or a block sequence. Testing
Turnout threshold A turnout alone does not create a Plant; multiple coordinated signals do. Direction chosen
Directional ABS A Line workflow generates and links ordinary Signal Masts. Testing
APB ownership Direction coordination needs an Owner above individual Masts. Constraint
Signal Line persistence Retain at least group provenance; decide later whether Line is a durable Facility. Open
Territory context ABS/APB/CTC remains territory-level behavior, not a per-Mast opening question. Direction chosen
