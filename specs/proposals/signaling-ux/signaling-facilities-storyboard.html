Signaling facilities — Mast, Plant, and Line

A clean storyboard for the facility-first direction: add a named facility using the existing entry point, show Bowties the railroad, and let Bowties derive the repeated signal relationships.

Current direction
Selected UX direction as of 2026-10-31: Signal Plant and Signal Line. Add Facility no longer offers Signal Mast separately. Masts remain the individual signals configured within a location or line; the Mast walkthrough below is configuration reference. Detailed workflow design and persistent Line or Territory ownership remain open.
Add facility Signal Mast Signal Plant Signal Line How they connect Decisions under test

Mockup scope (not product UI)

  • No hardware access or application occurs. Occupancy states, indication tables, Rulebook entries, and lamp assignments are illustrative, not validated mappings.
  • Selected controls preview choices, not a synchronized end-to-end plan. The Line's later signal counts, links, and connection tables retain the baseline example.
  • Both directions records operating intent only. Opposing signal views, two-ended external connections, and bidirectional/APB logic are not implemented.

Bowties already creates facilities by choosing a behavior template and entering a name. This mockup adds two signaling templates alongside the existing Block Indicator; the selected signaling template opens its dedicated wizard after the facility is added.

Design notes — not product UI

Behavior

  • One block is sufficient. Select existing named, detected blocks without renaming or rebinding them; missing detection is an incomplete prerequisite, not configured here.
  • Removing membership does not delete a shared block or detector. Reordering must preserve signal identities and bindings while previewing changed downstream references.
  • Direction count and coordination are separate. Both directions does not imply APB, and neither coordination policy is selected automatically.
  • Occupancy observations can update independently of draft edits; unavailable observations are not current state.

Mockup limitations

  • The three-row list is fixed; add, remove, and reorder controls are illustrative.

Open questions

  • Does the one-block path remain simple, including Line versus signal naming?
  • Bidirectional membership, opposite-end entrances, and section-level APB/CTC coordination need design without duplicating block identities or detection.

Design notes — not product UI

Behavior

  • Protected-block and internal downstream references derive from order. Only the final signal needs an external downstream reference.
  • Symbols show configured direction and protection, not live aspects or physical installation. Direction changes preserve names and bindings. Occupancy is separate; offline never presents retained observations as current.
  • Changing Line defaults preserves individual overrides. Occupancy-only Clear/Stop remains valid; Clear does not claim that an unmonitored path beyond the block is clear.
  • The endpoint opens existing connection controls without creating a Plant or signal. A Plant entrance remains Plant-owned and supplies a semantic restriction/Track Speed, not a literal aspect-name match or permission to enter the Line.

Open questions

  • Richer-vocabulary mappings, required downstream information, and unavailable-input behavior need validation. An unresolved reference must not imply Clear.

Design notes — not product UI

Behavior

  • Review differences against the existing configuration, including membership, resource releases, and changed links. Editing must not recreate unchanged signal identities.
  • Cancel discards draft edits. Saving a draft does not apply hardware configuration; existing configuration remains until application.
  • The operating summary describes intent, not validated delivery. Unanswered coordination stays explicit.
  • Failures must identify affected signals and prevent application of unvalidated dependent behavior.

Mockup limitations

  • Save/apply controls are disabled because checks remain pending.

Open questions

  • Line persistence, deletion, unavailable-state handling, and application/failure policy remain unsettled. Unvalidated dependencies must not be presented as safe to apply.

5. How the three facility scales connect

Ownership remains singular at the boundaries. A Mast may read a Plant entrance as its downstream signal, but it does not become part of two facilities.

Ownership rule

Signal Mast owns

One combined indication and its physical display.
  • Protected block
  • Optional local turnout
  • Next-signal cascade

Signal Plant owns

The coordinated behavior at localized trackwork.
  • Home signals
  • Routes and conflicts
  • Shared turnout bindings

Signal Line may own

The ordered relationship along plain track.
  • Block sequence
  • Generated Mast chain
  • APB direction coordination

6. Decisions this mockup is testing

The storyboard is useful before every domain name and persistence rule is settled. These are the specific conclusions a review should confirm or overturn.

Review guide
DecisionCurrent directionStatus
First question Choose a Signal Plant for a location or a Signal Line for plain track; no separate Mast entrance. Direction chosen
Plant threshold The protected location determines the Plant workflow, not whether it has one signal or several. Direction chosen
Directional ABS A Line workflow generates and links ordinary Signal Masts. Testing
APB ownership Direction coordination needs an Owner above individual Masts. Constraint
Signal Line persistence Retain at least group provenance; decide later whether Line is a durable Facility. Open
Territory context ABS/APB/CTC remains territory-level behavior, not a per-Mast opening question. Direction chosen
